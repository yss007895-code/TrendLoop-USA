How We Use Cookies on TrendLoop USA

Last updated: February 26, 2026

TrendLoop USA ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website trendloopusa.net. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — like your preferences and how you use the site — to make future visits more efficient and useful.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them manually).

Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may set their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Used to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: Used to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites. You can opt out via Google Ad Settings.
  • Amazon Associates: Affiliate tracking cookies are set when you click Amazon links on our site. These help attribute purchases for commission purposes. Amazon Cookie Policy

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies in the following ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies via their settings. Note that blocking all cookies may affect website functionality.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google Ad personalization: Visit Google Ad Settings to opt out of personalized advertising.
  • DAA opt-out: Use the Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-out Tool to limit interest-based advertising.

Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature that signals websites that you do not want to be tracked. Currently, there is no industry-standard interpretation of DNT signals, and our website does not respond to DNT signals differently than standard requests. We may update this policy if a standard is established.
